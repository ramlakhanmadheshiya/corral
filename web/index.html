An endless enclosure puzzle

Fence in empty space.
Bigger pens score more.

Drop chunky wall pieces on a 9 × 9 field. Wall in a patch of empty cells on all four sides and it's captured: it fills with colour like a paint bucket, scores its area squared, and the walls around it vanish. One big pen beats many small ones, if you dare to wait for it.

How it plays

Three pieces at a time. No rotation, no timer.

▦

Drag, drop, enclose

Drag one of three wall pieces onto the grid. Use all three and three more arrive. The edge of the board is open, so every pen needs walls all the way round.

9²

Area squared

A pen scores its area × area × 10: four cells make 160, nine make 810, sixteen make 2,560. A preview shows what a pen would score before you drop the piece.

◆

Gems and combos

A gem inside a pen doubles it, and gems stack. Close two pens with one piece for a combo. Later, rocks drop in and never vanish, and gems start to hop.

∞

Classic

Endless: chase your best score until none of your pieces fits. One continue per run, only if you want it. A fair generator always deals a piece that fits.

☀

Daily Board

The same rocks, gems and 30 pieces for everyone on the same day, built from the date. Score for stars, keep a streak, and share your painting.

★

120 challenges

Eight chapters of goals: a 16-cell pen, three gems, two pens at once, a score in a set number of pieces. Every one is played through by a bot, so it can be done.
