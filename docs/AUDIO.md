# Corral: audio

Every sound and both music loops are generated by `tools/synth_audio.py` (Python standard library only, fixed seeds, so reruns are identical). No samples.

| Sound | Where | How it's made |
|---|---|---|
| `tap` | Slider ticks, tutorial | Marimba-like pluck (C6), pitched up the major pentatonic scale in game |
| `click` | Every button | A short high pluck |
| `pick` | Picking up a piece | A small bubbly pop |
| `place` | Placing a piece | A wooden thunk: low pluck with a fast pitch drop plus a filtered noise click |
| `invalid` | A piece slides home | A soft falling triangle buzz |
| `pop` | Each wave of a pen filling | A pop with a fast pitch drop, pitched up the pentatonic scale per wave (16 steps) |
| `capture` / `big` | A pen closes (9+ cells: `big`) | Rising bells over a warm pad, with a breathy swell |
| `gem` | A gem is captured or appears | A fast sparkly bell arpeggio |
| `combo` | Two or more pens at once | A rising pluck arpeggio and a high bell |
| `vanish` | Walls disappear | A falling noise swell with three soft pops |
| `rock` | A rock drops in | Two low thunks |
| `hop` | A gem hops | A quick rising-falling sine |
| `gameover`, `complete`, `coin`, `unlock`, `error`, `open` | Results, rewards, cards | Bells, pads and swells (as in the earlier games) |
| `menu.wav`, `play.wav` | Music | Pentatonic bell melodies over pad chords, folded for seamless loops |

Headless runs (tests, CI) play nothing, so no audio objects are left at exit.
