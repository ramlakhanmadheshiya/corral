## Full description (4000 max)

Corral is a calm, colourful block puzzle with a twist: you don't clear lines, you fence in space.

Drag wall pieces onto a 9 × 9 field. When your walls close off a patch of empty cells on all four sides, it's captured: the pen fills with colour like a paint bucket, scores, and the walls around it vanish to free up room. The edge of the board is open, so every pen needs walls all the way round.

BIGGER PENS, FAR BIGGER SCORES
A pen scores its area squared: four cells make 160 points, nine make 810, sixteen make 2,560. So do you close a small pen now and stay safe, or hold out for a big one while the board fills up? A preview shows what a pen would score before you drop the piece.

GEMS, ROCKS AND COMBOS
• A gem inside a pen doubles it, and gems stack
• Close two pens with one piece for a combo
• In longer runs, rocks drop in that never vanish, and gems start to hop

THREE WAYS TO PLAY
• Classic: endless. Chase your best score until none of your pieces fits. Pieces get more awkward as you climb, but you are always dealt a piece that fits when any could.
• Daily Board: the same rocks, gems and 30 pieces for everyone today. Score for stars, keep a streak, and share your board.
• Challenges: 120 goals in 8 chapters, like "capture a 16-cell pen" or "close two pens at once" in a set number of pieces. Fewer pieces earn more stars, and every challenge has been played through so it can be done.

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Chunky walls, soft pastel colours, confetti, and a rising run of notes as each pen fills
• A hands-on tutorial that gets you capturing in seconds
• Daily missions, 39 awards, colour themes and pen styles
• Light and dark appearance, following your phone

PLAYER-FRIENDLY
• No timer, no energy, no lives
• Videos only when you choose them: one continue per run, or a hint when you're out of coins
• Progress stays on your device, with no account to make
